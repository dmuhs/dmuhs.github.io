Hi! My name is Dominik. I am an independent security researcher at [Kauz Security Services](https://kauz.gmbh/), where I do security code reviews, penetration tests, and general consulting work.

I have spent many years doing penetration tests and code reviews, and I am now working on the HTB Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS) certification.

To relax from breaking software, I build side projects, and some of them end up on the [Tools]({{< relref "/tools" >}}) page. I enjoy backend engineering in Python and Elixir, and these days also in Go and Rust. When I get the chance to leave the house, I take photos.

I'm also interested in:

- The security of smart contract systems
- Penetration testing and CTF contests
- Peer-to-peer network design and implementation

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/dmuhs/) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/lethalspoons.bsky.social). Come say hi!
