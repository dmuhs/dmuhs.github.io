---
title: "Frame"
host: "frame.spoons.fyi"
description: "Frames images with borders, blur, and camera data in your browser."
weight: 1
---

Frame puts a plain border, a blurred background, or a preset style around a photo. The styles can print the camera, lens, exposure, and date from the image's metadata, in looks such as a film strip or an instant print. It reads JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC files, one or many, and keeps images up to 4096 pixels on the longer side. Everything is drawn in the browser, so the images are sent nowhere.
