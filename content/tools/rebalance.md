The Cashflow Rebalancer works out how to split new money, such as a monthly saving, across your investments. You enter what each investment is worth now and the share of the portfolio it should have, and the calculator says how much to add to each, so the portfolio moves back to its targets without selling anything. The numbers are kept in the page address, so a calculation can be bookmarked or shared.
