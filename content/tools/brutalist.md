Brutalist is one small stylesheet for plain HTML. It weighs under 25 KB and needs no classes, no build step, and no JavaScript. It styles all standard HTML components, follows the visitor's color scheme, and comes with print styles, text modes for dense reading, margin notes, and typography for Japanese and Chinese. It is free to use under the MIT license. This site is built with it.
