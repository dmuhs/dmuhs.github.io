The Pomodoro Timer counts down rounds of focused work and the breaks between them. It starts with the classic rhythm of 25 minutes of focus and a 5 minute break, with a 15 minute break after every fourth round. Longer rhythms of 50 and 10 or 90 and 20 minutes are built in, and you can set your own lengths. In Flowtime mode you work as long as you like, and the break is a fifth of the time you spent. The timer can play a sound and send a notification when a round or break ends, start the next round on its own, and keep the screen awake while it runs.
