Favicons serves PNG icons of a single character, 180 by 180 pixels, at fixed addresses that any page can link to. Each icon comes with a dark and a light background and in a sans, a serif, and a mono font, and a page can link both backgrounds to follow the visitor's color scheme. Every font covers the visible ASCII characters, and sans also has 5,401 common Chinese characters. The icons are in the public domain under CC0 1.0. This site uses one as its own icon.
